Other initiatives favoring respect for our local environment include Little Bay Dredging,
Bike Rack Grant, Energy Performance Contract with schools, a response to DEP notice
of non-compliance regarding the Community Veterans Building, a Technical Assistance
Grant for Green Communities designation, and allowing an electric vehicle charging
station at the National Marine Life Center.
Website and IT
The Board approved of an inter-municipal agreement with Barnstable County for
information technology assistance to help with information technology, infrastructure,
strategic planning, vulnerability and security assessment.
Finances/Budget
FY22 Budget process was led by the town administrator creating and realizing a
structurally balanced budget. He presented the proposed budget January 12, 2021, to
the Board, followed by a separate meeting with the Finance Committee as outlined in
the Charter and Bylaws, subsequently releasing the budget to Finance Committee and
Board of Selectmen for continued distinctive review and vote.
Town Meetings and Elections
The Board supported the town clerk’s recommendation to move Precinct 2 to the
Community Veterans Building due to the Sagamore Fire Station not being conducive to
social distancing required during the COVID pandemic emergency orders.
The 15 person quorum was implemented during the State Emergency Orders and voted
by the Board. The Annual Meeting was moved from May to June 29th and was held on
Jackson Field at Bourne High School in order to accommodate social distancing and
remain outdoors.
The Board voted to hold a Special Town Meeting in November, limiting Articles, once
again, to those that were either deferred from the spring or were essential for business
because the COVID emergency restrictions were still in place.
Committees
Town government is supported by citizen engagement in 40 appointed committees and
a handful of elected boards. Many committees did not meet during COVID restrictions.
Mostly the ones that rcontinued to meet were the ones essential to town business. The
Cable Advisory Committee was re-established, and participants were selected to review
the cable contract with special counsel for the town. The Roadway and Traffic Safety
Committee was created to review more fully and more systematically, requests for signs
and traffic concerns.
