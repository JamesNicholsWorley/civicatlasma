ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION
OFFICIAL MINUTES
APRIL 10, 2021

The Annual Town Election was held at the Spring Street School on Saturday, April 10,
2021. Constable Bruce L. Holmquist, in accordance with the Massachusetts General Laws and
the By-laws of the Town of West Bridgewater, posted the Warrant, issued by the Board of
Selectmen on March 10, 2021.

Specimen ballots, cards of instruction and abstracts of the laws imposing penalties upon
voters were posted as required by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. Both precincts were set
up to maintain social distancing requirements and protect the health and safety of both the voters
and poll workers.

The polls were opened at 9:00 a.m. The voting machines in both precincts were
inspected and a zero total was printed out. A total of 1002(precinct 1) and 1000(precinct 2)
ballots were given to Wardens, Marilyn Raleigh and Peter Turner.

At the close of the polls at 8:00 p.m. the voting machines showed a total of 758
votes had been cast. 0 ballots were in the auxiliary boxes at the close of polls. 37 of the ballots
were Absentee Ballots. Due to Covid 19 voters were allowed to vote early by mail for this
election. We had 43 Vote By Mail Ballots. 678 voters voted at the polls on Election Day. The
total ballots cast was 758, which was 13.72% of the total registered voters in Town of 5523.
The following were sworn in as election officials:

Precinct I: Marilyn Raleigh, Warden; Marion Loughman, Clerk; Linda Kenrick,
Roberta Waite, Elaine Peckham, Diane Perry, Checkers; Sharon Ledin, Tally

Precinct 2: Peter Turner, Warden, Jane Hatch, Clerk; Christine Newman;
Sandra Irving, Louise Domingos, Joan McAndrew, Checkers; Sharon Trojano, Tally.

Katharine Turner; Greeter and Janice Golder; Closer

Jackson and Jefferson Roberts assisted with the setting up and taking down the election
equipment.
