DOINGS
TOWN OF MOUNT WASHINGTON
ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION RESULTS
TUESDAY MAY 13, 2025
The polls were declared open at 12:00 P.M. by David O. Whitbeck, Constable.
70 of the Town’s 137 registered voters cast their ballots. Poll workers were: David Whitbeck,
Constable; Linda Beckwith, Town Clerk; Elizabeth (Betsy) Garrett, Judy Whitbeck, Cory Hines,
Judy Isacoff, Betsy Spears, Beverly Dunn, and Dorothy Becker-Bonbrake, volunteers. Elizabeth
Lem was also present from 12:00 P.M. until 2:00 P.M.
There were no provisional or challenged ballots cast. There were eighty official ballots printed,
70 were cast and 10 unspoiled at the close of polls. There were also 3 specimen ballots printed
on yellow paper and posted in the Town Hall and 5 absentee ballots printed on green paper and
3 were cast and 2 unspoiled at the close of polls.
