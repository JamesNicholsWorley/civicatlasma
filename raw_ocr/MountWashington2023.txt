DOINGS
TOWN OF MOUNT WASHINGTON
ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION RESULTS
TUESDAY MAY 9, 2023
The polls were declared open at 12:00 P.M. by David O. Whitbeck, Constable.
94 of the Town’s 134 registered voters cast their ballots. Poll workers were: David Whitbeck,
Constable; Linda Beckwith, Town Clerk; Elizabeth (Betsy) Garrett, Virginia (Ginny) Filkins, Beverly
Dunn, Brian Tobin and Dorothy Becker-Bonbrake, volunteers. Elizabeth Lem was also present
from 12:00 P.M. until 2:00 P.M.
There were no provisional or challenged ballots cast. There were one hundred twenty-five
official ballots printed, 94 cast and 31 unspoiled at the close of polls. There were also 3
specimen ballots printed on yellow paper and posted in the Town Hall and 10 absentee ballots
printed on green paper and 6 were used and 4 unspoiled at the close of polls.
