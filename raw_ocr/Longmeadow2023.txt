TOWN OF LONGMEADOW
ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION MINUTES
LONGMEADOW COMMUNITY HOUSE

June 13, 2023

The qualified voters of the Town met at the time and place appointed. The ballots were
delivered to the polling place, the ballot boxes were shown to be empty, and the registers were
set at zero to the satisfaction of the Election Officers and the Police Officers on duty. The polls
were declared to be open at 7:00 a.m. Election Officers were as follows:
