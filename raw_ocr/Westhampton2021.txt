WESTHAMPTON -- ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION, JUNE 5, 2021

FROM THE TOWN CLERK'S REPORT, 2021 Annual Town Report, page 9:
Annual Town Election June 5, 2021- There were no races or questions on the ballot. Voter
turnout was a very low 93 out of 1,319 registered voters.
