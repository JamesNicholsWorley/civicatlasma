ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION
May 9, 2025

The polls opened at 12:00 noon and remained open until 7:00 P.M. at 18 Jacobs Road
Municipal Center. A total of 82 out of 563 voters turned out to cast a ballot. This is
15% of the total number of registered voters. Of the 82 ballots cast 11 were vote by mail
ballots.

School Committee - 3 year seat :
The 3-year seat on the School committee had no candidate put forth; there were several
write in candidates resulting in a tie. Under Mass General Law Chapter 53 Section 53
when there is a tie in a local election it is considered a “failure to elect’; it will be up to
the Select Board to appoint a member or leave the seat vacant until the next regular
election.
