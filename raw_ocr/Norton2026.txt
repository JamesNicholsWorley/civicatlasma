Minutes of the Annual Town Election
April 11, 2026

The Annual Town Election was held on Saturday, April 11, 2026. Polling
hours were from 8:00a.m. through 4:00p.m. The Warrant was properly
posted. The Town did not have the option for Early Voting for this election;
however Absentee Ballots were accepted thru Friday, April 10, 2026.

The ballot boxes were opened by Town Clerk, Jill Seppa, Election Warden
Ruth Schneider and Assistant Town Clerk Stella Young. The ballot boxes
proved to be empty and the Zero Tabulation tapes were run and displayed
in the Town Hall foyer. Sergeant Jacob Dennett were present for election
security and proceedings.

The Specimen Ballot, Instructions to Voters, Voters Bill of Rights, and Zero
Tabulation tapes from Precinct 1, Precinct 2, Precinct 3, Precinct 4, and
Precinct 5 were posted, The AutoMark handicap voting machine was turned
on and tested.

The polls were declared open at 8:00a.m. by Town Clerk, Jill Seppa. There
were 6 people waiting when the polls opened.

Attest:

Thank you to the head of Emergency Management Ray Cord for creating a
traffic logistics plan and implementing alongside Finnian DePont, Melanie
Towle, Tracy DeBruyn, Becky Bedard, Rick Towle, John Silva, Joe Giordano.

Thanks to the Norton Town Hall Highway Department for assisting in
setting up the polling equipment.

At the time of this Annual Town Election, there was a total of 15,316
registered voters in Norton. There were a total of 1232 votes cast, making
an 8% total turnout.

Absentee Ballots were processed by the Warden Ruth Schneider during the
day.

At 4:00pm, Town Clerk Seppa and Election Warden Ruth Schneider closed
the polls. The voting machines were programmed for tabulation and three
sets of tapes were run. Election workers reconciled their books and write-in
votes were recorded. Unofficial results were ready by 5:30 p.m.

All materials were secured in the vault with security oversite provided by
Sergeant Jacob Dennett Town Hall vault at 4:30 pm.

The Town Clerk would like to thank all the staff members who worked at
this election to make the day run smoothly. A special thank you to Ruth
Schneider, Donna Fong, and Assistant Town Clerk Stella Young. Norton and |
as the Clerk, are remarkably fortunate to have such a dedicated community
of Election Workers that implement such a knowledgeable, safe and
smooth election process.

Congratulations to all candidates, Denise Luciano Select Board member,
Frank Kearns, Rob Balazs and Chris Momaney as the Planning Board
members, James Chabot as the Board of Assessors member, Justine
Callanan as the School Committee member, and John "Jack" Conway as the
Town Moderator.
