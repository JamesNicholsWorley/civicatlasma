Acting in pursuant of a warrant issued by the Select Board, Ludlow, Massachusetts notifying all those qualified to vote
for the election of candidates for office. The voters in Precinct 1 met in the Harris Brook Street Elementary School on
Fuller Street; Precinct 2 at Paul R. Baird Middle School, Rooney Road; Precinct 3, Ludlow High School, Chapin
Street; Precinct 4 at East Street School, East Street; Precinct 5 at First Church in Ludlow, Center/Church Street;
Precinct 6 at Paul R. Baird Middle School, Rooney Road.
A total of 783 (5% of 16,386 registered voters) ballots were cast. Precinct 1 — 135; Precinct 2 — 87; Precinct 3 — 140;
Precinct 4 — 133; Precinct 5 — 131; Precinct 6 — 157; the polls were open from 10:00 a.m. until 8:00 p.m.
