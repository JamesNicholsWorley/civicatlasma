Town of Boylston
Annual Town Election — May 13", 2024
Notice to Town of Boylston Voters was given and Specimen Ballots were posted as
required by law. The voting machine was tested at least three (3) days prior to Election
as required by law. Voters of the Town of Boylston met at Hillside Gymnasium, Town
Hall, 221 Main Street, for the Annual Town Election according to legal notice. The ballot
box was examined by the Warden and the Assistant Town Clerk in view of the
Constable. The polls were declared open by the Clerk at 1200 hours and were closed
at 2000 hours.
There are 4,273 registered voters in Boylston; 518 turned out to vote. The counter on
the ballot box, computer printout of results, and checklists concurred that 518 ballots,
including absentee ballots, were cast.
