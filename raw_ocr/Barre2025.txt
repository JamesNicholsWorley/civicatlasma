Summary

This is a nonbinding advisory question to determine if the Town of Barre supports the acquisition by the Town of the
Old Reservoir Dam and related property located near Flaherty Road along the Prince River, for purposes of
repairing, replacing and maintaining the dam or removing it so as to be able to protect public safety and to regulate
water levels of the Old Reservoir. The Old Reservoir Dam is in poor condition and has been deemed a High Hazzard
Dam by Massachusetts Office of Dam Safety, which means that the Dam is at high risk of failure and that failure
would be likely cause damage to property in the vicinity of the Dam. The Town of Barre does not own the Dam and
has no ability to address the problem. Options for repair, replacement or removal of the Dam have been assessed.
The Commonwealth has offered fund construction costs, but the Town will incur property acquisition and annual
maintenance costs if it acquires the Dam.

A YES vote on this question means that you are in support of the Select Board pursuing Town ownership of the Dam
by requesting an appropriation and other necessary votes at Town Meeting. A yes vote DOES NOT require that the
Select Board take any particular action and no actions will be taken until all proper Town Meeting authorizations
have been obtained.

A NO vote means that you are not are not in support of the Select Board pursuing Town ownership of the Dam and
the current conditions will remain in effect."
