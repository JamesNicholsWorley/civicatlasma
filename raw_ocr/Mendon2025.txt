Annual Town Election
May 13, 2025, proceedings
Pursuant to the foregoing warrant the Annual Town Election was held on Tuesday May
13, 2025, at the Miscoe Hill School gymnasium. Polls opened at 7:00 am. Kathy Rich and
Leigh Martin served as Wardens. The greeters were Cheryl Lemon, Jeff Kroesen, and
Diane Howell. The tellers were Alison Chu, Nancy Fleury, Nancy Phipps, Barbara
Goddard, Sue Edmondson, Peg Tetreault, and Kathy Alexander. Clerks were Carol Cook,
Ruth O’Grady, Ann Vandersluis, and Wayne Phipps. The officer on duty was Staff Soft.
Guy Kloczkowski. Polls closed at 8:00pm. 669 votes were cast. Results were announced
and posted at 8:05 pm by Town Clerk Ellen Agro.
