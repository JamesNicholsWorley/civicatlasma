Town Meetings & Elections
ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION — May 14, 2024, proceedings
Pursuant to the foregoing warrant the Annual Town Election was held on
Tuesday May 14, 2024, at the Miscoe Hill School gymnasium. Polls
opened at 6:57 am. Kathy Rich and Leigh Martin served as Wardens. The
greeters were Jeff Kroesen, Carol Saari, Mary Ellen Dean, and Debra
Schmitt. Tellers were Rory Crisfield, Nancy Phipps, Barbara Goddard,
Sue Edmondson, Peg Tetreault, Leesa Michael, and Kathy Alexander.
Clerks were Carol Cook, Ruth O’Grady, Ann Vandersluis, and Wayne
Phipps. The officers were duty officers and Phil Dunleavey.
Polls closed at 8:00pm. 1,240 votes were cast. Results were announced
and posted at 8:10 pm by Kathy Rich.
