The meeting was called to order by Jodie J. Paradis, Town Clerk at 12:00 P.M. and the reading of the
warrant was waived in absence of the Moderator with no objections. The polls were opened promptly at 12:00
P.M. and closed at 8:00 P.M. Athanasia Fox was sworn in as Warden by the Town Clerk. Board of Registrars
Deana Ridgway and Alice Taverna present for meeting. The following election officers were swom in by the
Town Clerk.

At the opening of the polls and before any ballots were cast, the Ballot Box register indicated 0000 and
at the close of the polls the Ballot Box register indicated 227. The officers in charge of the voters list counted
and announced the whole number of names on said list to be 227. The presiding officer then removed the ballots
from the ballot box and caused them to be counted one by one and announced the whole number of ballots to be
227. The ballots having been duly sorted and counted were recorded and declared in open meeting to be as
herein after set forth. Voters check list checked with total votes. The elections officers were dismissed and —
meeting adjourned without further delay at 10:30 P.M. The results of the ballots cast were as follows:
