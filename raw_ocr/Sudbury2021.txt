2021 Annual Town Election
March 29, 2021
The Annual Town Election was held at two locations. Precincts 1-1A, 2 & 5 voted at the Fairbank
Community Center, 40 Fairbank Road, and Precincts 3 & 4 voted at the Town Hall, 322 Concord
Road. The polls were open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. There were 2,223 votes cast, representing
16.19% of the town's 13,728 registered voters. The final tabulation was done at the Town Hall.
