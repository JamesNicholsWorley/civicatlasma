June 2, 2025 Annual Town Meeting
At the Annual Town Meeting, held on June 2, 2025, at which a quorum was present, the
following votes were taken:
Non-voters present were Tammy Crockett - Monty Tech, Aaron O’Connor - Petersham Center
School, Peter Cross - Mahar Regional School Committee, Michelle Tontodonato - Mahar
Regional School, Tom Brown- Monty Tech, Greg Vine - Athol Daily News, Chief Peter Buck,
Gianni Nicoletti and Hazel Richards
A vote was taken to allow the non-residents to speak as needed.
A vote was taken to allow the Moderator to call the 2/3 votes.
Article 1: The Town voted to receive the reports of the Town Officers and Committees. Reports
were read by Susan Dougherty on behalf of the Board of Selectmen and Richard Cavanaugh on
behalf of the Advisory Finance Committee. The Town voted to accept the reports as read.
The Town voted to allow the Moderator to vote Articles 2-18 under the Consent Calendar.
Article 2: The Town voted to authorize the Treasurer to enter into compensating balance
agreements during Fiscal Year 2026, as permitted by Massachusetts General Laws (MGL),
Chapter 44, Section 53F.
Article 3: The Town voted to use the income of the William B. Spooner Fund as of March 31 of
the current fiscal year at the Petersham Memorial Library for Fiscal Year 2026.
Article 4: The Town voted to instruct the Treasurer to use the Elizabeth Newton Charities Trust
Fund and Elizabeth Newton Deserving Poor Trust Fund for assistance to Petersham veterans
approved for benefits by Massachusetts Department of Veteran’s Services through the Local
Veterans’ Service Office for Fiscal Year 2026.
Article 5: The Town voted to allow the Conservation Commission to use the accumulated
interest from the Evelyn V. Murphy Conservation Fund as of March 31 of the current fiscal year
for Fiscal Year 2026.
Article 6: The Town voted to allow the Conservation Commission to use the accumulated
interest from the Lewis and Corrine Babbitt Wildlife Sanctuary Fund as of March 31 of the
current fiscal year, to be used at the Sanctuary for Fiscal Year 2026.
