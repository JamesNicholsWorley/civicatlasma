As usual the budget season arrived in early January. This year the work went very smoothly
through the beginning of May. In fact, an early Annual Town Meeting was planned for June 3,
2024, just after the annual town election. There were 2 candidates for the position of
selectman. Aaron Langlois ran for re-election and Michael Brunelle ran as a write-in
candidate. This was the only contest on the ballot. Michael Brunelle won the seat by 17
votes. Aaron filed for a recount since this vote was so close and there were some questions
on the use of stickers for the write-in. Michael was declared the winner following the
recount.

As the year was winding down three we noted that there were three outstanding area that
still need to be addressed or completed: the Finance Committee does not have enough
members for a quorum for the incoming fiscal year, the roads of Long View Estates are not
yet ready to be accepted as town roads and the future of the Town Hall is still in question
and will be for the foreseeable future.
