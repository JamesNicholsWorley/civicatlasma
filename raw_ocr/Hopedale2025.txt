Annual Town Election
May 13, 2025
Hopedale, Massachusetts

In accordance with the posted warrant, for the Annual Town Election, the inhabitants of the
Town of Hopedate, qualified to vote in elections met in the Draper Gymnasium at 12:00 pm
with Warden Ellen Murphy in charge of the election.

At 8:00 pm the polls were closed. The number of ballots agreed with the checklist, and it was
announced that the number of ballots cast was three hundred forty-three (343) was 7.69%. At
the time of the election there were four thousand four hundred sixty (4460) inhabitants
registered to vote.

The absentee ballots were processed during the polling hours. There were four (4) absentee

ballots mailed and four (4) were returned. Three hundred thirty-nine (339) residents voted at
the polls.
