June 3, 2024
At the Special Town Meeting, at which a quorum was present, I certify that the following votes were
taken:
Article 1: The Town voted to take the sum of $38,000 from Free Cash and $15,000 from Police
Department Fund excess for a total sum of $53,000 to eliminate the Fiscal Year 2024 Fire Department
Account deficit.
Article 2: The Town voted to take the sum of $11,000 from Free Cash to eliminate the Fiscal Year 2024
Transfer Station Account deficit.
Article 3: The Town voted to take the sum of $5,500 from Free Cash to eliminate the Fiscal Year 2024
Town Telephone Account deficit.
Article 4: The Town voted to take the sum of $3,450 from Free Cash and $2,000 from the Fire Inspection
Overlay Account for a total of $5,450 to eliminate the Fiscal Year 2024 Highway Department Salaries
deficit.
Article 5: The Town voted to take the sum of $3,782 from Free Cash to eliminate the Fiscal Year 2024
Unemployment Compensation Account deficit.
The meeting was adjourned at 7:23pm.
