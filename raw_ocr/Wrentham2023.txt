Minutes of the Annual Town Election
April 3, 2023

The Annual Town Election was held on Monday, April 3, 2023. Polling hours were from 7:00
a.m. through 8:00 p.m. The Warrant was properly posted.

The Town did not have the option for Early Voting or Vote by Mail for this election, however,
Absentee Ballot applications were accepted until noon the Friday before. The last day to
register to vote for this Election was Friday, March 24*,

The polls were declared open at 7:00 a.m. by Warden Mary Geromini. There were two voters
who arrived as the polls opened. The Specimen Ballot, Instructions to Voters, Voters Bill of
Rights, and Zero Tabulations from Precinct 1, Precinct 2, Precinct 3 and Precinct 4 were posted.
The AutoMark handicap voting machine was turned on.

Many special thanks to the Elementary School custodian staff and the Town’s DPW and facilities
department. Also to the Wrentham Police Department for working the detail (Sergeant Dan
Morris, Officer Derrick Cassidy, Officer Jonathan McAlister and Officer Bart Folan.

1

The ballot boxes were opened by Sergeant Morris and Town Clerk, Cynthia Thompson. The
ballot boxes proved to be empty and the zero tabulation tapes were run and posted. The keys
were handed over to Officer Cassidy.

The turnout was slow and steady throughout the day. At the time of this Annual Town Election,
there were a total of 9,452 registered voters in Wrentham. There were 15 requests for
Absentee ballots. This election had a total of 984 voters just making it 10% total turnout.
Absentee Ballots were processed by the Precinct Clerks during the day. There were no issues
with the ImageCast tabulators and zero residents took advantage of the Automark machine.
There were no affirmations, no provisional ballots and just a couple of spoiled ballots.
At 8:00 p.m. Town Clerk Thompson closed the polls. The voting machines were programmed for
tabulation and two sets of tapes were run. Tellers reconciled their books and write-in votes
were recorded. Unofficial results were ready by 9:25 p.m. There were Board of Assessors,
Constable and Planning Board vacancies that no nomination papers were pulled, so required
write-in votes.
All materials were returned to Town Hall by 10:30 p.m. and official results were posted on the
Town’s website.
The Town Clerk would like to thank all the staff members who worked at this election to make
the day run smoothly. Special thanks to Assistant Town Clerk, Ann Fisk for all her great
assistance in this successful election. /
