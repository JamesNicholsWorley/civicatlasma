DOINGS AT THE MAY 19, 2026 ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION
Polls were declared open at 9am by Warden, Stanley Kostek. Checkers were Sharon Parsons, Lorraine Herbert, Diane
Bonneau and Carol Kostek. Ballot Box: Kathy Banas and Sloane Spanknebel Clerk: Carol Kostek and Joanne Pliska
Constables/Police: Jesse Green, Haley Grandeese, Gabrial Chambers and Jacob Laughlin.
The ballot machine tape total was 359. There was one (1) spoiled ballot. (25) EV’s and (5) AV’s. A total of 359 voted out
of an eligible 3973 voters = 9% turn out. (This was an uncontested race election with no ballot questions)
