Proceedings of the Town Elections
May 17, 2021
R. Christopher Brittain, Town Clerk, declared the polls open at 7:00 am. Zero report was printed
from the Image Cast tabulator and posted at 5:51:28am.
Election workers on staff throughout the day were Kathy Bort, Maryann Antoniazzi, Lucy
Boldyga, Estella Ortiz Bodnar, Ann O’Brien, Jim Eithier, Sarah Navin, Elizabeth Mead, Marcia
Slaminsky, Mary Swift, Jane Burns, Laura Page, Jan Holmes, Mary McDarby, Teresa Navin.
These workers included substitutes needed due to the COVID-19 health crisis.
Police Officer Wood was on staff throughout the entire day.
Polls were declared closed at 8:00 pm. Results were printed from the Image Cast machine at
8:05:52pm. 983 total ballots were cast on the image cast machine.
