COMMONWEALTH OF MASSACHUSETTS
TOWN OF GOSNOLD
WARRANT
ANNUAL TOWN MEETING
May 20, 2024
County of Dukes County, ss.

To either of the Constables of the Town of Gosnold:
GREETINGS:

In the name of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts you are hereby
directed to notify and warn the inhabitants of the Town of Gosnold qualified to
vote in town affairs and elections to meet at the Avalon in said Gosnold on
Monday, May 20, 2024 at 10:30 in the forenoon to act on the following articles:
Article 1: To choose a moderator to preside at said meeting.

Leo Roy elected unanimously.
Article 2: To hear the report of the Town Officers and act thereon.
Article 3: To elect the following officers for the ensuing terms and set
salaries for the same in the below amounts, or such other
amounts as the Town determines.
a. One Selectboard member, Board of Health member for 3
years.
Salary $7,179.00
Win Sanford elected unanimously.
b. Two School Committee members for 3 years.
Salary $125.00
Paula DiMare and Renata Davidson elected unanimously.
c. Two Public Library Trustees; one for 2 years, one for 3 years.
No Salary.
Lisa Wright and Jane Chalton elected unanimously.
d. Two Harbor Committee members for 3 years.
No Salary.
Ralph Cataldo and Paula DiMare elected unanimously.
Article 4: To see if the Town will vote to authorize the Treasurer, with
approval of the Board of Selectmen, to borrow money from
time to time in anticipation of the revenues for the financial
year beginning July 1, 2024, in accordance with the Provisions
of General Laws chapter 44, section 4.
Approved unanimously.
4 4

Article 5: To see if the Town will vote to authorize the Treasurer to enter
into compensating balance agreements for the financial year
beginning July 1, 2024 in accordance with Provisions of
General Law’s chapter 44, section 53F.

Approved unanimously.

Article 6: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate a sum of money to
defray town charges and expenses for the fiscal year ending
June 30, 2025.

Approved unanimously.

Article 7: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate a sum of money in
anticipation of reimbursement of the Commonwealth’s share
of the cost of work under Massachusetts General Laws,
chapter 90, section 34.

Approved unanimously.

Article 8: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate a sum of money
for expenses of the Harbor Management Committee.
Approved unanimously.

Article 9: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate a sum of money
for the Water Enterprise Fund.

Approved unanimously.

Article 10: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate an amount of
money for the State indemnity for pest and disease control in
accordance with the Provisions of General Laws, chapter 132,
section 14.

Approved unanimously.

Article 11: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate and transfer a sum
of money into the Stabilization fund, or take any other action
thereon.

Approved unanimously.

Article 12: To see if the Town will vote to transfer $150,000 from
General Fund Stabilization to Electric Light to defray
maintenance and repairs costs for Fiscal Year 2024 due to
lightning strikes and equipment issues, or take any other
action thereon.

Approved unanimously.
5 5

Article 13: To see if the Town will vote to transfer the sum of $12,000
from Harbor Stabilization to the General Fund for the purpose
of purchasing a new motor for the town boat, or take any other
action thereon.

Approved unanimously.

Article 14: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate $60,000 as its
matching share of a grant of $300,000 from the Seaport
Economic Council for permitting and design of the dredging
project adjacent to the marina, or take any other action
thereon.

Approved unanimously.

Article 15: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate the sum of $44,000
for the purpose of Permitting the West End Pond from Town
Stabilization.

Approved unanimously.

Article 16: To see of the Town will vote to transfer $6567.00 from Water
Stabilization to the Water Reserve Fund for the annual
contribution to the USDA Rural Development or take any
other action thereon.

Approved unanimously.

Article 17: To see if the Town will vote to appropriate the sum of money
for the purpose of paying old bills from FT 2023, or take any
other action thereon.

Approved unanimously.
