The polls were opened at twelve o’clock in the afternoon (12:00 NOON) by the
Temporary Moderator, Janet L. Weidner, and the Temporary Moderator declared the
meeting dissolved and the polls closed at eight o’clock (8:00 PM). Of the One Thousand
Sixty-Eight (1068) registered voters eligible to vote in the Annual Town Election as of
the last day to register to vote on May 15, 2021, Three Hundred and One (301) voters
cast ballots. Forty-five (45) Early Mailed Ballots were requested, of which Forty-Three
(43) were received and cast by the close of polls at 8PM. Five (5) Absentee Ballots were
requested of which Five (5) Absentee Ballots were received and cast by the close of polls
at 8PM. The results were tallied as follows:
