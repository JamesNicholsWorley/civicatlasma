Annual Town Election
May 12, 2026
Hopedale, Massachusetts

In accordance with the posted warrant for the Annual Town Election, the qualified voters of
the Town of Hopedale convened at the Draper Gymnasium, Polls opened at 7:00 a.m. with
Warden Ellen Murphy presiding over the election.

The polls closed at 8:00 p.m. Following the close of voting, the ballot count was verified
against the checklist and found to be accurate. A total of one thousand eighty-seven (1,087)
ballots were cast, representing 27.03% voter participation. At the time of the election, there
were four thousand twenty-one (4,021) registered voters in the Town of Hopedale.

Fifty-one (51) vote-by-mail ballots were processed during polling hours.
