TUESDAY MAY 12, 2026

The polls were declared open at 12:00 P.M. by David O. Whitbeck, Constable.

Whitbeck, Constable; Linda W. Beckwith, Town Clerk; Elizabeth (Betsy) Garrett, Judy
Whitbeck, Cory Hines, Dorothy Becker-Bonbrake and Beverly Dunn, volunteers.

There were no provisional or challenged ballots cast. There were fifty official ballots
printed, 48 were cast and 2 were unspoiled at the close of polls. There were 3 specimen
ballots printed on yellow paper and posted in the Town Hall.
