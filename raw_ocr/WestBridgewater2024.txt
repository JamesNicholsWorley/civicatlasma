## ANNUALTOWNELECTION OFFICIALMINUTES APRIL13,2024

The Annual Town Election was held at the SpringStreet School on Saturday, April 13, 2024.Constable Patrick J. Galligan, in accordance with the Massachusetts General Laws and the By-laws of the Town of West Bridgewater,posted theWarrant,issued by theBoard of Selectmen on March 11, 2024.

Specimen ballots, cards of instruction and abstracts of the laws imposing penalties upon voterswereposted asrequiredbytheCommonwealthof Massachusetts.

The polls were opened at 9:00 a.m. The voting machines in both precincts were inspected and a zero total was printed out.A total of 739(precinct 1) and 739(precinct 2) ballots weregiventoWardens,ChristineNapoleon andPeterTurner.

At the close of the polls at 5:00 p.m. the voting machines showed a total of 546 voteshadbeencast.5ballotswereintheauxiliaryboxesatthecloseofpollsandwere handcoounted.17oftheballotswereAbsenteeBallots.302ballotswerevotebymail.232 voters voted at the polls on Election Day.The total ballots cast was 551,which was 9% of the totalregisteredvotersinTownof5960.

Thefollowingweresworninaselectionofficials:

Precinct 1: Christine Napoleon, Warden; Roberta Turner, Clerk; Sandra Irving, Louise Domingos, Joan McAndrew, Checkers; Paul Golder, Closer; Sharon Trojano, Tally

Precinct 2: Peter Turner, Warden, Jane Hatch, Clerk; Elaine Peckham; Linda Kenrick, Frances Deibel, Checkers; Janice Golder, Closer; Kathleen Grant, Tally.

SharonLedinprocessedVotebyMail ballots.

Jackson and Jefferson Roberts assisted with the setting up and taking down the election equipment.
