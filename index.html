Browsing opens the file list on GitHub; the files themselves are served from this site at the same paths. Files are named by stem — the municipality without spaces, then the year: Athol2023.json, Athol2023_d0.pdf. The _d0 suffix numbers the documents tried for a town-year; a _d1 means a second, genuinely different document. The pre-2021 records cite their annual report by URL rather than mirroring the PDF.

Reading a record

Fields ending _original are transcribed from the document exactly as printed, including clerk typos and inconsistent office names. They are never edited, because every check works by finding them in the source. Corrections are recorded separately rather than overwriting what the document says.

Blanks, Others and Write-ins are tally rows, not people. A votes of null means no count was printed; the status field says whether the office was uncontested or won on write-ins. num_winners is the number of seats up for election, not the number of people who won.

Scope

Annual municipal elections only. Special elections and preliminaries are collected but not published here. State, county and federal races are out of scope even where they appear on the same ballot.

Coverage is near-complete from 2021. The 2000–2020 records are drawn from annual town reports and cover 208 municipalities, not all 351 — the map says so where it shows them.

If you find an error, the most useful thing you can send is the town, the year, and what the document actually says.
