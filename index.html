Massachusetts Civic Atlas

Annual municipal election results 1,890 town-years · 1,555 source documents

Massachusetts holds hundreds of municipal elections a year and nobody collects the results. Each clerk publishes them however they like — a PDF, a scan, a page on the town website, sometimes only a local newspaper — and a year later the link is often gone. This gathers them in one place and keeps the evidence.

Files are named by stem — the municipality without spaces, then the year: Athol2023.json, Athol2023_d0.pdf. The _d0 suffix numbers the documents tried for a town-year; a _d1 means a second, genuinely different document.

How to read a record

Fields ending _original are transcribed from the document exactly as printed — including clerk typos and inconsistent office names. They are never edited, because every check works by finding them in the source. Corrections are recorded separately rather than overwriting what the document says.

Blanks, Others and Write-ins are tally rows, not people. A votes of null means no count was printed — the status field says whether the office was uncontested or won on write-ins.

Caveats worth reading

This is a work in progress and it is better to say so than to imply otherwise. Coverage is incomplete. The quality-assurance system that checks these records against their source documents is being rebuilt, and while many records have been verified line by line, others have not. Where a figure could not be confirmed against a document, the aim is to record that rather than present the whole set as settled.

Annual municipal elections only. Special elections and preliminaries are collected but not published here. State, county and federal races are out of scope even where they appear on the same ballot.

If you find an error, the most useful thing you can send is the town, the year, and what the document actually says.
