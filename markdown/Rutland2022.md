Special Town Meeting Minutes
__________
Town of Rutland
Special Town Meeting
Thursday, November 18, 2021
6:00 P.M.
Glenwood Elementary School
65 Glenwood Road
The meeting was opened at 6:01pm by Moderator Randy Jordan. Town Clerk Anita Carlson read the
posting of the warrant, followed by a salute to the flag. Present were Finance Committee members
Karen Nahrwold, Darren Ross, Thomas Ruchala, Peter O’Malley, Lynn Wiinikka, Ted Purcell and
Alexander Mowatt, Town Counsel Lauren Goldberg of K.P. Law, Town Administrator Ron San
Angelo and Select Board members Leroy (Skip) Clark, Leah Whiteman, Carol Benoit and Lyndon
Nichols, along with Assistant Town Clerk Daymian Bartek, who administered the electronic voting
system. A test of the remote voting clickers was held prior to the start of voting. One hundred
registered voters were present.
ARTICLE 1:
Karen Nahrwold, Finance Committee Chair, moved that the Town vote to transfer a sum of
$50,828.60 of Certified Free Cash to the Town’s Stabilization Account.
Motion seconded Finance Committee Recommended Favorably
In response to a question from Louis Cornacchioli, 18 Blueberry Lane, Karen Nahrwold said the
current balance in the Stabilization Fund is $1,049,892.00 and the amount in the Capital
Stabilization Fund is $154,992.00
Yes 62 No 3 Abstain 0 Motion Carried
ARTICLE 2:
Karen Nahrwold moved that the Town vote to transfer a sum of $95,828.60 of Certified Free
Cash to the Town’s Capital Stabilization Fund.
Motion seconded Finance Committee Recommended Favorably No Discussion
Yes 55 No 1 Abstain 0 Motion Carried
ARTICLE 3:
Karen Nahrwold moved that the Town vote to transfer a sum of $50,828.60 of Certified Free
Cash to the Town’s OPEB Trust Fund.
Motion seconded Finance Committee Recommended Favorable
Yes 71 No 1 Abstain 2 Motion carried

ARTICLE 4:
Karen Nahrwold moved that the Town vote to transfer $76,736.00 from Free Cash, to be used as
an additional funding source for the FY2022 operating budget approved under Article 4 of the
June 5, 2021 Annual Town Meeting.
Motion seconded Finance Committee Recommended Favorably
Yes 79 No 3 Abstain 2 Motion carried
ARTICLE 5:
Karen Nahrwold moved that the Town vote to transfer $15,563.00 from Free Cash for the
purpose of funding COLA raises for non-union and non-contracted employees for the FY 2022
fiscal year, as well as Holiday Pay for the non-union Police Chief consistent with the Town’s
contractual obligations; and to include additional compensation for the Town Clerk and the
Town Planner according to the compensation wage matrix.
Motion seconded Finance Committee Recommended Favorably
Tim Nahrworld, 149 Pommogussett Rd., speaking as a citizen, moved that the Town amend
article #5 for the purpose of funding stipends that were cut in this year’s budget by transferring
from Free Cash to the following line items in the Fiscal Year FY 2022 Operating Budget
approved under Article 4 of the June 5, 2021 Annual Town Meeting.
Moderator 001-114 $ 75.00
Select Board 001-122 $3000.00
Board of Assessors 001-141 $1500.00
Planning Board 001-175 $2500.00
Board of Health 001-510 $ 750.00
Motion Seconded
The Town Administrator spoke in favor of the amendment
Vote on amendment Yes 61 No 12 Abstain 7 Amendment carried
Vote on article with amendment Yes 73 No 3 Abstain 3 Moderator declared
article with amendment carried
ARTICLE 6:
Karen Nahrwold moved that the Town vote to transfer $30,000.00 from Free Cash for the
purpose of funding Town costs as they relate to 300th Anniversary Celebrations.
Motion seconded Finance Committee Recommended
The meeting body had questions regarding how this article relates to article # 26 which is a
