# Leverett, Massachusetts — Annual Town Meeting election of officers, May 3, 2025 (winner-only)

Source: Daily Hampshire Gazette, "Regional school budget, gift of woodlot approved at Leverett's Town
Meeting," May 5, 2025. Leverett elects its officers at Town Meeting (no ballot). Roughly 100 residents
attended. Uncontested; winner-only.
