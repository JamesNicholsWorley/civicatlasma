DRACUT – In one of the most hotly contested races for the Board of Selectmen in years, incumbent Joseph DiRocco prevailed in a tight race over Phil Thibault in Saturday’s town elections.
Unofficial results, at 8:40 p.m. Saturday had DiRocco, the town’s former fire chief, with 1,176 votes and Thibault with 1,042 votes
For two seats on the School Committee, challenger Rebecca Duda topped the ticket with 1,494 votes compared to 1,430 for incumbent Joseph Wilkie. Rosemary Espinal finished out of the money with 564 votes.
Thibault, an local architect and Planning Board member, has run for selectman more than a half-dozen times and several times against DiRocco.
Going into Saturday’s vote, Thibault had momentum but it appeared to fall just short.
Thibault, however, is closely associated with the controversial Dracut Connections cable television show on Dracut Access Television, which over the last several years has personally attacked many local officials.
DiRocco used the connection to his advantage, but still appeared on the show a couple weeks before election day with Selectmen Chairman Jesse Forcier.
DiRocco was criticized for abandoning the town manager search process and hiring insider chief financial officer Ann Vandal last June. That move had many Dracut insiders, like former Selectman Warren Shaw, supporting Thibault, despite his association with Dracut Connections.
It was also significant that Selectman Tony Archinski didn’t endorse his colleague, but instead supported Thibault.
Forcier, however, supported DiRocco and warned that if Thibault was elected, he would join with Archinski and force Vandal out.
Thibault previously ran in 2005, 2007, 2015, 2016, 2018, and 2020. He ran against DiRocco in 2015 and 2018.
