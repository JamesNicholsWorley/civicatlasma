Polls were declared open at 9am by Warden, Stanley Kostek.  Checkers were Linda Wiesner, 
Jason Kostek, Dianne Bonneau, Carol Kostek, Sharon Parsons, Janet Hukowicz, Lorraine 
Herbert and Mary Cadorett  Ballot Box:  Helen Baj (honorary post)  Clerk:  Joanne Pliska

provisional ballot accepted.
