# East Brookfield, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Election, May 10, 2022

Source: the town's own results posting, an image of the OFFICIAL BALLOT with the
Town Clerk's turnout figures written on it by hand, recovered from the Internet
Archive at
https://web.archive.org/web/20260601181927id_/https://www.eastbrookfieldma.us/sites/g/files/vyhlif541/f/news/results_for_webpage_0.jpg

The image is 923x1200 and was read with OCR column by column, at 5x to 8x
magnification; the heading and turnout lines were confirmed from five independent
crops and page-segmentation modes.

The offices and candidates appearing on the ballot for the annual town election
held Tuesday, May 10, 2022 are as follows. This document is the ballot, not a
tally: it prints no vote count for any office, so no count is recorded here.

No office has more candidates than seats.

## Offices with no candidate printed on the ballot

Three offices carried only a write-in line, with no name printed:

| Office | Term | Seats |
|---|---|---|
| Board of Selectmen | three years | 1 |
| Finance Committee | three years | 1 |
| Tree Warden | one year | 1 |

Because the ballot prints no name and no count for these three, no winner is
recorded for them.

## Turnout

The Town Clerk's figures, written on the ballot image:

- 99 ballots cast
- 1,647 registered voters
- 6 percent turnout
