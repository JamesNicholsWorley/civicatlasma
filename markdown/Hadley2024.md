Question 1 Ladder Truck 
 
Shall the Town of Hadley be allowed to exempt from the provisions of proposition two and one-half, so-
called, the amounts required to pay for the bond issued in order to pay the costs of a new Ladder Truck for the 
Fire Department and for all the costs related and incidental thereto? 
                     
Yes received seven hundred seventy three votes    773 
No received four hundred sixteen votes     416 
      Blanks      76 
        Total              1265
