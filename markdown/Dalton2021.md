TOWN OF DALTON
ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION 
MAY 10, 2021
The Election was held from 11:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. at the Dalton Community House, pursuant to the Warrant on page 1.  1,000 Official Ballots were delivered to the Warden, and the Election Supervisor  certified that the Ballot Boxes were empty and set at zero.  At the conclusion of the counting and tabulation, the Town Clerk announced that there had been a total of 523 Ballots cast as follows:
