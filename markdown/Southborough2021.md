# Southborough, Massachusetts — Annual Town Election, May 11, 2021 (official results)

Source: Southborough Town Clerk official results images (via mysouthborough.com, "Official town
election results posted," May 17, 2021). Only the two CONTESTED races were published as result
tables; other offices on the ballot were uncontested. Totals are town-wide across Precincts 1–3.

## Board of Selectmen — 3-year term (1 seat)
- Andrew Dennington — 462 (winner)
- Michael D. Weishan — 439
- All Others — 1
- Blanks — 12
- Total ballots cast: 914

## Northborough/Southborough Regional School Committee — 3-year term (2 seats)
Combined votes (Northborough + Southborough voters) decide the seats; Southborough-only subtotals in parentheses.
- Kathleen M. Howland — 1747 combined (Southborough 514) — winner
- Karen A. Ares — 1573 combined (Southborough 379) — winner
- Mariam Ibrahimi — 1555 combined (Southborough 394)
- Write-ins — 17 combined
- Blanks — 1148 combined
- Southborough ballots cast in this race: 1828 (two-seat, votes ×2)
