ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION MINUTES
This Annual Town Election, called by the Board of Selectmen as required by law on a Warrant duly
posted by Constable Benjamin J. Nickerson on March 27, 2023 was held on Thursday, May 11,
2023 at the Chatham Community Center (702 Main Street). The polls opened at 7:00 a.m.
Town Clerk Julie Smith and Assistant Town Clerk Amy Burrow examined the ballot boxes,
demonstrated the boxes were empty and showed the ballot counters to be registered at zero.
The Optical Scan Electronic Voting Systems operated throughout the day without incident.
Inspectors Charleen Greenhalgh, Sandi Porter, Louise Redfield, Diane Rowlings, Dee Shippelhute,
Jennifer Smith, and Melanie Taylor all contributed to the success of this election.
The polls closed at 8:00 p.m. At the time of this Election there were 5,945 registered voters in
Chatham, 1,118 ballots were cast (19%).

QUESTION FOUR (4)
Charter Amendments Approved by 2022 Annual Town Meeting
Shall the Town of Chatham approve the Charter amendment proposed by the Town Meeting as
summarized below?
Section 8-2 Periodic Charter Review
At least once every five years In each year ending in a zero, beginning with the year 2030, a special
committee shall be appointed by the select board for the purpose of reviewing the provisions of
the charter and shall have the title of charter review committee. The charter review committee
shall submit a report to town meeting concerning any proposed amendments or revisions to the
charter which such committee deems necessary.
(Strikethrough indicates language proposed for deletion).
(Underline indicates language proposed).
Summary: This Charter language change will extend the required Charter review period from five
years to ten years beginning in 2030 and clarifies when the review period begins.
A YES vote will authorize the change in the Charter review period.
A NO vote will leave the Charter unchanged.
