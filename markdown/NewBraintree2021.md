# New Braintree, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Election, Monday, May 3, 2021

Source: the Town of New Braintree's own results table, posted as an image to
Facebook,
https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=4112845612087879&set=a.307064331466227

The accompanying post reads:

"The Annual Town Election was held on Monday, May 3, 2021. Out of 790 voters, 60
ballots were cast, representing 7.5% voter turnout. All results are also posted
on the website as well."

## Turnout

60 ballots cast of 790 registered voters. The town's percentage is sound:
60 / 790 = 7.59 percent.

This figure is not taken on the caption's word alone. It is confirmed by the
table itself -- see the arithmetic check below.

## Arithmetic check: the table proves its own turnout

Because this table counts blanks as a row, every office must sum to exactly the
number of ballots cast, and a two-seat office to twice that. Every one of the nine
offices does:

    Selectboard            55 + 5                      =  60
    Assessor               55 + 5                      =  60
    Board of Health        57 + 3                      =  60
    Finance Committee      14 + 46                     =  60
    Constable              58 + 2                      =  60
    Planning Board, 4 yr   57 + 3                      =  60
    Planning Board, 5 yr   46 + 14                     =  60
    Cemetery Commissioner  30 + 26 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1     =  60
    Library Trustee, 2 seats  52 + 39 + 8 + 21 = 120   =  60 x 2

Nine independent sums, all landing on 60. The ballots-cast figure is therefore
established by the table and does not rest on the caption; the caption supplies
the registered-voter total of 790, which the table cannot check.

## Contested and unopposed

Seven of the nine offices were uncontested: a single name, elected, with the
balance blank.

Two were not.

  * Library Trustee, two seats, three names. Judith Webb 52 and Christine Maio 39
    were elected; Kelly Hitt 8 was not.
  * Cemetery Commissioner drew no printed candidate at all. Thirty of the sixty
    ballots were left blank, and the remaining thirty went to five write-ins, led
    by Kelly Hitt with 26. The table records the counts and does not state whether
    Hitt was seated; that is not asserted here.

Kelly Hitt appears in both of those races, on the same ballot.
