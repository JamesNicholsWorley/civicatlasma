# Douglas, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Election, 5/13/2025

**UNOFFICIAL.** The Town of Douglas published these figures to Facebook and nowhere else; the post captions itself `Unofficial results of 5/13/2025 Town Election`. Douglas has printed no Annual Town Report for 2023, 2024 or 2025, and multiple public-records requests for the certified 2025 return were still open as of 2026-08-19. This file is a transcription of that post as relayed by Rebecca Lavallee, archivist at the Douglas public library, and supplied by the project owner on the same date. It is not a certified return and must be replaced by one if the clerk ever issues it.

The post is dated May 14, 2025 -- the day it was written. The election was Tuesday 5/13/2025, which the post itself gives twice.

Total registered voters in Douglas: 7790

Total voters at May 13, 2025 election: 204

Precinct 1 total: 67
Precinct 2 total: 67
Precinct 3 total: 70

The turnout printed in the post, .03 percent, is a typo; 204 of 7790 is 2.62 percent. The derived figure is not stored.

## Corroboration -- who is actually serving

The vote counts above come from an unofficial post. Who they elected does not: whoever won in May 2025 holds the seat now, and the town's own rosters are independent of the post. Checked 2026-08-19.

- **Board of Selectmen** (https://douglas-ma.gov/directory.aspx?did=22) -- Timothy P. Bonin (Vice Chairman) and Leslie M. Stevens (Chairman) both sit -- the two winners of the two 2025 Selectmen openings. Harold R. Davis also sits, consistent with his 2023 win already in the corpus.
- **Planning Board** (http://douglas-ma.gov/Directory.aspx?did=34) -- Jacob Gniadek and Michael Zwicker both sit -- the two winners of the two 2025 Planning Board openings.
- **School Committee** (https://douglas.ss13.sharpschool.com/school_committee) -- Beth Bergstrom (Vice Chair) and Becki DeMarco (Secretary) both sit, both with terms expiring May 2028. Heather Morin expires 2026 and Meg Rodzen and Julia Lagerholm expire 2027, so the two 2028 seats are the 2025 class. This is what settles the second seat.
