# Richmond, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Election, Saturday, May 21, 2022

Two sources, neither sufficient alone.

The ballot is from The Berkshire Eagle, "Familiar faces, and no competition, mark
this year's Richmond town election", 15 April 2022,
https://www.berkshireeagle.com/news/central_berkshires/familiar-faces-and-no-competition-mark-this-years-richmond-town-election/article_daaf33ea-bce8-11ec-a81b-db80802be17c.html

The turnout is from the Town Clerk's report in Richmond's own 2022 Annual Town
Report, page 71 (Massachusetts State Library, Richmond-2022.pdf).

The results of the annual town election held Saturday, May 21, 2022 are as
follows. There were no contested races: every name on the ballot was a current
office-holder seeking another term, nominated and approved at the annual town
caucus. The source names each candidate and prints no vote count for any office,
so no tally is recorded here.

## Officers elected, all unopposed

| Office | Term | Elected |
|---|---|---|
| Selectman | three years | Neal Pilson, the current board chairman, for a third term |
| Board of Health | two years | Andrew Fisher |
| Board of Health | three years | Louise Maron |
| Finance Committee | three years | Patricia Callahan |
| Library Trustee | three years | Nancy McConnell |
| Town Moderator | one year | William Martin |
| Planning Board | five years | Katherine Keenum |

Polls were open from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. at Town Hall on State Road.

## Corroboration from the town's own record

Richmond's 2022 Annual Town Report, page 6, Directory of Town Officials, carries
every one of these people with a term-end year consistent with election in 2022:
Pilson 2025, Fisher 2024, Maron 2025, Callahan 2025, McConnell 2025, Martin 2023.

One discrepancy is recorded rather than resolved. The Eagle says Katherine Keenum
was elected to a FIVE-year Planning Board term, which from 2022 would expire in
2027; the town's directory lists her Planning Board term as expiring 2024. The
office and the person agree across both sources; only the term length does not,
and neither source is obviously the more reliable on that point.

## Turnout

From the Town Clerk's report, page 71:

"May 21, 2022 Annual Town Election 55 out of 1256 voters attended (0.04%)"

55 ballots cast of 1,256 registered voters. Note that the percentage printed in
the report is wrong: 55 of 1,256 is 4.4 percent, not 0.04 percent. The two counts
are the figures relied on here. The same page records the annual and special town
meetings of 18 May 2022 at 291 of 1,256, and a special town meeting on 16 November
2022 at 15 of 1,281.
