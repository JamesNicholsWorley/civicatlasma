# Kouchakdjian, Gargeya Win Select Board Race, Sues, Morton, Ryan Take School Committee Seats

<!-- image -->

[By Nancy Brumback](https://sudburyweekly.com/author/user60/)

April 1, 2025

Flag of Usa

<!-- image -->

##### Share This Article

Share Post

In Monday's Annual Town Election, Lisa Kouchakdjian and Radha Gargeya were elected to the Select Board in a contested race for the two seats.

Elizabeth Sues was elected to the two-year seat on the preK-8 Sudbury School Committee (SPS), filling a vacancy created by a resignation.

Charles Morton and John Ryan won the two seats on the Lincoln-Sudbury (L-S) Regional District School Committee.

According to the Town Clerk's website, there were 2,398 votes cast, 17% of the town's 14,214 registered voters. That's down from last year's town election, when there were 3,375 votes cast, 25% of the town's 13,420 active registered voters.

In unofficial results posted by the Town Clerk Monday night, Gargeya, a former member of the Lincoln-Sudbury school committee, received 1,671 votes to lead the Select Board race. Select Board incumbent Kouchakdjian received 1,598 votes in her bid for re-election.  Kevin Matthews, currently a member of the Lincoln-Sudbury school committee, received 902 votes.

In the Sudbury School Committee race, Sues received 1,255 votes.  Allison Vanderels received 848 votes. Both were first-time candidates.

Both Sudbury and Lincoln held elections Monday for the two Lincoln-Sudbury school committee seats. John Ryan, a former member of the L-S school committee, topped the vote count with 2,097 votes-1,528 from Sudbury and 569 from Lincoln. Charles Morton, a first-time candidate, received 1,949 votes-1,436 from Sudbury and 513 from Lincoln. Eric Poch, currently a member of the Sudbury finance committee, received 936 votes-836 from Sudbury and 100 from Lincoln.

Detailed election results by precinct are available on the Town Clerk's [website](https://sudbury.ma.us/election/321/) . Lincoln results are [here](https://www.lincolntown.org/DocumentCenter/View/98343/ATE-33125-Unofficial-Election-Results-PDF) .

Here are the vote totals for the candidates in uncontested races, in ballot order:

**Board of Assessors** Trevor Haydon: 1,681

**Goodnow Library Trustee** Katina Fontes: 1,616 Jill Caseria: 1,556

**Board of Health** Carol Bradford: 1,810

**Park &amp; Recreation Commissioner** William Granger Atkeson: 1,575 Mara Huston: 1,606

**Planning Board** Anuraj Shah: 1,523 Kirsten Roopenian: 1,570

**Sudbury School Committee, three-year seat** Jessica McCready: 1,692

*Nancy Brumback is a freelance reporter who lives in Sudbury.*
