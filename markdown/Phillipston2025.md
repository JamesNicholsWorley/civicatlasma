# Phillipston, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Election, May 19, 2025

Transcribed from `Tallie Ballott May 19, 2025.xls`, the Town Clerk's tally workbook, attached to the Clerk's 19 May 2025 meeting on mytowngovernment.org -- the town's official Open Meeting Law minutes host. **Sheet `02` only.** Sheet `03` of the same workbook is a candidate list with no votes on it, carried forward from 2002 and re-saved every year; nobody on it stood in 2025.

Office and candidate spellings are the document's own.

**110 Ballots Cast.** Every race below sums to 110.

Registered voters: 1,448. Source of that figure: the Town Clerk's FY25 annual report narrative, which gives 1,448 registered and 110 cast; the tally sheet itself prints only the ballots-cast figure.

Date: May 19, 2025. The tally sheet prints no date; the workbook is attached to the Town Clerk's meeting of 19 May 2025 on mytowngovernment.org and is titled Tallie Ballott May 19, 2025; the FY23 annual report states the ballot vote is held on the third Monday in May, and 19 May 2025 is that Monday.
