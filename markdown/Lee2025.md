# Lee, Massachusetts — Annual Town Election, May 12, 2025 (uncontested; write-ins for two seats)

Source: The Berkshire Edge, "Lee election results confirm uncontested races, add write-ins to School
Committee, Housing Authority," May 13, 2025. 150 of 4,835 registered voters cast ballots (3% turnout).

## Town Moderator — 1-year term
- Matthew D. Carlino (incumbent) — 138 (winner)
- Write-ins — 3
- Blanks — 9

## Select Board — 3-year term
- Gordon Bailey (incumbent) — 148 (winner)
- Write-in — 1
- Blanks — 1

## Planning Board — 5-year term
- Davis C. Forrest — 138 (winner)
- Blanks — 12

## School Committee (two open seats; won by write-in — one seat remains unfilled)
- Dorothy French (write-in) — 22 (elected)
- Blanks — 228

## Housing Authority (won by write-in)
- Kathy DeVarennes (write-in) — 31 (elected)
- Thomas Logsdon (write-in) — 5
- Other write-ins — 8
