HINSDALE — Newcomer William Goddard was elected via write-in to a vacant Finance Committee seat at the annual town election on Saturday.
Goddard defeated Janice Senner 15-6 to secure the two-year seat. Both were running as write-in candidates.
Out of the town’s 1,689 registered voters, 93 cast ballots — a turnout of about 5.5 percent, according to results provided by Town Clerk Dawn Frissell.
The two-year Finance Committee seat was the only one of four vacant positions filled at the election. Other positions that remain unfilled include a two-year assessor term, a three-year Planning Board seat and a four-year Planning Board term.
Anyone interested in filling the positions can apply to the Select Board, Frissell said.
Several incumbents were reelected, including Raymond Huntoon to a three-year Select Board term and Frissell as town clerk. Both Huntoon and Frissell were running unopposed for their respective positions.
Other incumbents reelected include James Chivers, Tracey Lussier and Mary Rice to three-year seats on the Finance Committee and Laura Galliher to a three-year assessor term. Incumbent Kore Longdyke was also reelected to a 5-year Planning Board term.
