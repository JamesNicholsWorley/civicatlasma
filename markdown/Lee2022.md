cast on the image cast machine.

Total ballots cast was 1092 out of 4477 registered voters (24.39% voter turnout). There were 15 spoiled ballots.  
 
 
Hand counts and write ins were tallied at the polling location. The used and unused ballots were sealed in separate containers and 
used ballots returned to Town Hall at 9:30 pm.  Results were posted on the town website and sent to media outlets at 10:30pm .
