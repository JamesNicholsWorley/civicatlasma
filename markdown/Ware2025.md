# Ware, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Election, Monday, April 14, 2025

Unofficial election results, published by the Town of Ware at
https://cms1files.revize.com/warema/Unofficial%20Results%20Annual%20Town%20Election%20Aprl%2014,%202025.pdf

The document is a two-page scan with no text layer. It was rendered at 400 dpi
and read with OCR; the name/vote pairings below were reconstructed from word
coordinates, not from reading order, because the two columns are far enough apart
that plain OCR emits every name first and then every number.
