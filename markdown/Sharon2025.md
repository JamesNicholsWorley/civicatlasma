ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION 
MAY 20, 2025 
 
Vote By-Mail/Absentee Voting 
 
The Absentee system began on January 21, 2025, when the first request for an 
Absentee ballot was processed at the Town Clerk’s Office. The Vote By-Mail 
system began on February 12, 2025, when the first application for a Vote By-Mail 
ballot was processed at the Town Clerk’s Office. The application process was 
available until 5:00 P.M. on May 13, 2025. The first ballots sent through the United 
States Postal Service were mailed on April 29, 2025. The final ballots sent through 
the mail went out on May 14, 2025. In total, 55 Absentee/Accessible Ballots were 
sent, and 161 Mail-In Ballots were packaged/mailed.

May 20, 2025

Provisional Ballots Tallying 
 
3 Provisional Ballots were cast by the close of the polls on Tuesday, May 20, 2025.  
After researching all the Provisional Ballots 2 qualified to be counted. Those who 
cast Provisional Ballots were informed of their ballot’s status.

of opening and hand counting the qualified Provisional Ballots, meeting the

Kendra Uma Kodira and Beth L. Lappen. The opening concluded at 9:06 A.M.
