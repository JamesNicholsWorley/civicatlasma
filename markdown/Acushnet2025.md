Ballots cast: 996. That figure is the newspaper's; the registered-voter and turnout boxes on the clerk's sheet are blank. The highest tally on the sheet is 756, which is consistent with it.

## Four offices drew no candidate

Commissioner of Trust Funds (full term and the unexpired term ending April 27, 2027), Trustee of Free Public Library (two seats) and Park Commissioner all read TBD on the sheet. The newspaper independently reports these as having no name on the ballot, and notes that "some write-ins still need to be counted, and will likely decide some of those races". This record cannot say who won them and does not guess.

## Deliberately not recorded

The debt exclusion for up to $4.2 million of school air-quality work, YES 229 / NO 754 -- a ballot question, out of scope. The sheet's per-precinct figures for it sum to exactly the totals the newspaper printed, which is a useful check that the two documents describe the same election -- but a ballot question is not a race and is not landed.
