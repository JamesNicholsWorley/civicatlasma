ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION
OFFICIAL MINUTES
APRIL 8, 2023
The Annual Town Election was held at the Spring Street School on Saturday, April 8,
2023. Constable Patrick Galligan, in accordance with the Massachusetts General Laws and the
By-laws of the Town of West Bridgewater, posted the Warrant, issued by the Board of
Selectmen on March 8, 2023.
Specimen ballots, cards of instruction and abstracts of the laws imposing penalties upon
voters were posted as required by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
The polls were opened at 9:00 a.m. The voting machines in both precincts were
inspected and a zero total was printed out. A total of 1061 (precinct 1) and 1060(precinct 2)
ballots were given to Wardens, Christine Napoleon and Peter Turner.
At the close of the polls at 8:00 p.m. the voting machines showed a total of957
votes had been cast. 62 of the ballots were Absentee Ballots. The total cast on election cast in
person at the polls was 895. The total ballots cast was 957, which was 16.8% of the total
registered voters in Town of 5686.
The following were sworn in as election officials:
Precinct 1: Christine Napoleon, Warden; Roberta Turner, Clerk; Sandra Irving, Diane
Perry, and Joan McAndrew: Checkers. Sharon Ledin; Closer. Paul Golder, Tally
Precinct 2: Peter Turner, Warden; Jane Hatch, Clerk; Louise Domingos,
Linda Kenrick and Katharine Turner; Checkers. Janice Golder; Closer. Kathleen Grant, Tally
Jackson and Jefferson Roberts assisted with the setting up and taking down the election
equipment.
