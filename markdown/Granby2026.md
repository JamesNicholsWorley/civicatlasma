GRANBY, Mass. (WWLP) – Voters in the town of Granby went to the polls Monday for the town’s annual election.
There were three contested races on the ballot, a two-way race for a single seat on the Select Board, a three-way race for the Board of Health, and a four-way race for three seats on the Board of Library Trustees.
Here are the results for the contested races (incumbents denoted with an asterisk *):
- Select Board
- Jeffrey Lawson – 488
- Rosemarie Rosen – 387
 
- Board of Health
- Mael Walkowiak – 539
- Richard Bombardier* – 301
 
- School Committee (2 seats)
- Trevor Augustino* – 599
 
- Write Ins – 18
- Thomas Hamel – 4
- Tim Abbott – 2
- Ro Rosen – 2
 
- School Committee (one-year unexpired term)
- Write Ins – 42
- Thomas Hamel – 3
- Tim Abbott – 2
 
- Library Trustees (3 seats)
- Paula Beaulieu* – 555
- Candace Ribiero* – 518
- Angela Chagnon – 427
- Nita Abbott* – 409
