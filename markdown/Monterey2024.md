# Monterey, MA — Annual Town Election, May 13, 2024

Source: The Berkshire Eagle, "Kevin West defeats Myles Pierce for Monterey tree warden",
by Heather Bellow, May 13, 2024.
https://www.berkshireeagle.com/news/southern_berkshires/monterey-tree-warden-town-elections/article_171214c8-113e-11ef-b282-0b725d634382.html

Turnout: 19 percent of the town's 795 registered voters.

## Tree Warden (one-year term) — CONTESTED
| Candidate | Votes |
|---|---|
| Kevin West | 132 |
| Myles Pierce | 17 |

Tree warden was the only contested seat in this election.
