# East Brookfield, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Election, Tuesday, May 9, 2023

Source: a photograph of the town's own OFFICIAL BALLOT for the 9 May 2023 annual
town election, used by the Town Clerk as a tally sheet, posted as an image to
Facebook,
https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=249019854319048&set=a.176214564932911

## What the document is

This is not a results press release. It is the printed official ballot itself --
headed "OFFICIAL BALLOT / ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION / EAST BROOKFIELD, MASSACHUSETTS /
MAY 9, 2023", bearing the town seal, the standard instructions to voters, and a
Town Clerk's signature -- on which the clerk has written each office's vote count
by hand in ink beside the candidate's name, highlighted the winners in yellow
marker, and totalled the turnout at the foot.

Every figure below was read directly off that image at magnification, one row at
a time. Handwriting is not OCR-safe and the machine transcription of this sheet
was wrong in several places, so no number here is taken from OCR and none is
derived by arithmetic from another number.

## Turnout

Written by hand across the bottom of the sheet:

"297 VOTERS CAST BALLOTS
 1658 REGISTERED VOTERS
 18% VOTER TURNOUT"

297 ballots cast of 1,658 registered voters. The clerk's percentage is sound:
297 / 1658 = 17.9 percent.

The digit in "1658" is written over a correction and was checked at high
magnification. It reads 1658. Both 1658 and a possible 1638 would round to the
18 percent the clerk wrote, so the percentage cannot be used to arbitrate it; the
reading is from the stroke itself.

## The two regional school district seats

East Brookfield and Spencer form the Spencer-East Brookfield Regional School
District, and both towns vote on both of the district's seats. For these two
offices the clerk ruled two columns in the right margin and headed them "EB" and
"SP", recording each town's count separately.

East Brookfield representative, three years, vote for one:

| Candidate | East Brookfield | Spencer | Elected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patrick L. Lacaire | 266 | 1,043 | yes |

Spencer representative, three years, vote for one:

| Candidate | East Brookfield | Spencer | Total | Elected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Christopher Lee Woodbury | 155 | 604 | 759 | yes |
| Catherine A. Knowles | 42 | 238 | 280 | no |
| Melinda Lutzen O'Connor | 68 | 349 | 417 | no |

One reading is flagged rather than asserted flatly. Lacaire's Spencer figure is
written as "SP: 1043" on the ballot's WRITE-IN line beneath his name, not in a
column. It is read here as Spencer's vote for Lacaire, because the clerk used the
identical EB/SP convention on the race directly below it and because 1,043
write-ins in a town of Spencer's size is not a possible reading. That is an
inference about placement, not about the digits, which are unambiguous.

## Four offices nobody won

The Collector, the two-year Finance Committee vacancy, the Planning Board seat
and the Tree Warden each carry the clerk's handwritten words "Failure To Elect"
on the write-in line. No candidate was printed for any of them and no write-in
reached the threshold. Whether these were later filled by appointment is not
stated by this source and is not asserted here.

## Arithmetic checks

Every count on the sheet is consistent with 297 ballots cast:

  * Treasurer, the closest race: 139 + 148 = 287, nine short of 297 with blanks
    making up the rest. Allen won by 9.
  * Board of Health: 215 + 74 = 289.
  * Spencer representative, East Brookfield's own column: 155 + 42 + 68 = 265.
  * No single-seat figure anywhere on the sheet exceeds 297.

## Two offices, one person

Jennifer R. McNeaney appears twice, elected as Cemetery Commissioner and as
Trustee of Shade Trees and Cemetery Funds, with 267 votes in each. Both are
separate offices on the ballot with separate ovals; the identical count is what
the sheet records and is not a transcription error.
