This Annual Town Election, called by the Board of Selectmen as required by law on a Warrant duly 
posted by Constable John Proudfoot on March 24, 2025, was held on Thursday, May 15, 2025, at 
the Chatham Community Center (702 Main Street).  The polls opened at 7:00 a.m. 
 
Assistant Town Clerk Amy Burrow  and Warden Anita Doucette examined the ballot boxes, 
demonstrated the boxes were empty and showed the ballot counters to be registered at zero.   
Although I was assured by multiple state agencies that I was legally allowed to participate in this 
Election because I would be implementing and carrying out my statutory responsibilities I chose 
not to, as my husband, longtime Harbormaster Stuart F.X. Smith was on this ballot as a candidate 
for Select Board .  W e were fortunate to have seven (7) Town Clerks from the following towns 
rotate into the polling location through the day (each holding an approximately 2 -hour stint) 
during Election Day to lend their support in my absence.  These Town Clerks include Barnstable 
Town Clerk Ann Quirk, Brewster Town Clerk Colette Williams, Dennis Town Clerk Theresa Bunce, 
Eastham Town Clerk Linda Sassi, Orleans Town Clerk Kelly Darling, Wellfleet Town Clerk Jennifer 
Congel, and Yarmouth Town Clerk Mary Maslowski. 
 
The Optical Scan Electronic Voting Systems operated throughout the day without incident.   
Inspectors Charleen Greenhalgh, Bette Hahner, Sandi Porter, Diane Rowlings, Dee Shippelhute, 
Jennifer Smith, Rachel Smith, Paula Wiseman, and Melanie Taylor all contributed to the success 
of this election. 
 
The polls closed at 8:00 p.m.  At the time of this Election there were 6, 136 registered voters in 
Chatham, 1,565 ballots were cast (26%).

QUESTION TWO (2) 
Non-Binding Public Opinion Advisory Question  
To Cease Gaseous Discharge of Radioactively and Chemically Contaminated Industrial 
Wastewater in Cape Cod Bay 
 
Whereas, Holtec, owner of the Pilgrim Nuclear Power Station in Plymouth, is discharging 
untreated radioactively and chemically contaminated industrial wastewater into the 
atmosphere; and,  

 
Whereas, these airborne pollutants are being carried by prevailing winds over and into Cape Cod 
Bay and our communities; and,  
 
Whereas, just as liquid discharge into Cape Cod Bay is in violation  the Massachusetts Ocean 
Sanctuaries Act and other state laws, this gaseous discharge also violates the Ocean Sanctuaries 
Act and other state laws Holtec agreed to follow; and,  
 
Whereas these airborne pollutants threaten public and environmental health and safety -
including the safety of Holtec’s own workers – and our marine-based economy;  
Therefore, shall the people of the Town of Chatham direct the town government to communicate 
with Governor Maura Healey, Attorney General Andrea Campbell, the State Legislature, and all 
other relevant authorities to employ all means available to ensure tha t the law is enforced and 
to ensure further that Holtec immediately cease the gaseous discharge of the radioactively and 
chemically contaminated industrial wastewater at Pilgrim?
