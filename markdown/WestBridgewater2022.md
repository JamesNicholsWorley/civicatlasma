ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION
OFFICIAL MINUTES
APRIL 9, 2022
The Annual Town Election was held at the Spring Street School on Saturday, April 9,
2022. Constable Bruce L. Holmquist, in accordance with the Massachusetts General Laws and
the By-laws of the Town of West Bridgewater, posted the Warrant, issued by the Board of
Selectmen on March 4, 2022.
Specimen ballots, cards of instruction and abstracts of the laws imposing penalties upon
voters were posted as required by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
The polls were opened at 9:00 a.m. The voting machines in both precincts were
inspected and a zero total was printed out. A total of 987(precinct 1) and 987(precinct 2) ballots
were given to Wardens Marilyn Raleigh and Peter Turner.
At the close of the polls at 8:00 p.m. the voting machines showed a total of 812
votes had been cast. There was 1 provisional ballot which was accepted and counted in the
Clerk's Office on Tuesday, April 12, 2022. 60 of the ballots were Absentee Ballots. The total
ballots cast was 813, which was 14.5% of the total registered voters in Town of 5607.
The following were sworn in as election officials:
Precinct 1: Marilyn Raleigh, Warden; Marion Loughman, Clerk; Elaine Peckham,
Christine Newman, Christine Napoleon and Roberta Tuner, Checkers; Sharon Ledin, Greeter.
Precinct 2: Peter Turner, Warden, Jane Hatch, Clerk; Louise Domingos, Sandra Irving,
Joan McAndrew, and Frances Diebel, Checkers; Sharon Ledin, Greeter.
Janice Golder and Sharon Trojano, Tally.
Jackson and Jefferson Roberts assisted with the setting up and taking down the election
equipment.
The election proceeded as follows:
