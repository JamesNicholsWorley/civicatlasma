# Alford, MA -- Annual Town Election, Tuesday 17 May 2022

Source: Town of Alford, `news/election_results.pdf`, linked from the town's news
item "Annual Election Results", **posted 18 May 2022 at 10:58am** -- the morning
after the election.
https://www.townofalford.org/sites/g/files/vyhlif6926/f/news/election_results.pdf

**How the year was established.** The scan's header reads "List of Candidates
nominated to be voted for in the Town of Alford May 17, 202" -- the last digit of
the year is cropped off the page. It is NOT inferred from the calendar: the news
item that links this file is stamped "Posted on: May 18, 2022". (17 May 2022 was
a Tuesday, and Alford votes on a Tuesday in May -- 18 May 2021, 21 May 2024 --
which is consistent but is not what the year rests on.)

The document is a scan with no text layer; the figures below are from OCR at 600
dpi, where the vote counts are TYPED, not handwritten, and read cleanly.
