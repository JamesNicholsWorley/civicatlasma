Tuesday’s election was also marked by a clean sweep by the “Kids First” School Committee ticket that Mazzarella endorsed.
Perhaps the biggest winner of the night for the ticket was Salvatore Perla Jr., who earned the largest number of votes (3,494) in a four-way race for three at-large seats on the School Committee.
Re-elected as at-large members were Brandon Robbins (3,352) and Melissa Bible (3,121).
Fellow incumbent Suzanne Koehler will leave the board in January after coming in fourth place with 2,980 votes.
Other “Kids First” candidates won in Ward 1, where Gregory Renchkovsky beat Natalie Stassen by just 14 votes, 654-640;
Ward 3, where Greg Thomas easily beat fellow first-time candidate Ray German 972-414;
and Ward 4, where Josh Bowdridge ran unopposed.
