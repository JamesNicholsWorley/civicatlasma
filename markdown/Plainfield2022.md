# Plainfield, MA -- Annual Town Election, Saturday 7 May 2022

Source: Town of Plainfield Annual Report 2022, printed page 6, "RESULTS OF FY 2023
TOWN ELECTiON May 7, 2022". Held at Plainfield Town Hall on Main Street, polls
open 1 to 6 p.m.
https://archives.lib.state.ma.us/server/api/core/bitstreams/9acf85b4-dae2-4583-b7b5-e146cefacc42/content

The report calls this the "FY 2023" election because it seats officers for fiscal
year 2023; the election itself was held on 7 May 2022.

**Ballots cast: 96 registered voters came out to vote.**
Ten uncontested candidates and two ballot questions.
