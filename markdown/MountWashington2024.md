DOINGS
TOWN OF MOUNT WASHINGTON
ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION RESULTS
TUESDAY MAY 14, 2024
The polls were declared open at 12:00 P.M. by David O. Whitbeck, Constable.
83 of the Town’s 139 registered voters cast their ballots. Poll workers were: David Whitbeck,
Constable; Linda Beckwith, Town Clerk; Brian Tobin, Acting Chief of Police, Virginia (Ginny)
Filkins, Elizabeth (Betsy) Garrett, Judy Whitbeck, Lesliann Furcht, Cathy Torrico, Cory Hines
and Beverly Dunn were volunteers.
There were no provisional or challenged ballots cast. There were ninety official ballots printed
on white paper, 77 cast and 13 unspoiled at the close of polls. There were 10 absentee ballots
printed on purple paper and 6 were cast and 4 unspoiled at the close of polls. There were 3
specimen ballots printed on green paper and posted in the Town Hall.
