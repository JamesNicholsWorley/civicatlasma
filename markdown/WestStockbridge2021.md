# West Stockbridge, Massachusetts — Annual Town Election, May 10, 2021 (uncontested; counts reported)

Source: The Berkshire Eagle, "Kavanagh reelected to Select Board in uncontested West Stockbridge
elections," May 11, 2021. No contested races; 82 residents cast ballots. Eleven offices filled.

## Results
- Select Board, 3-year term — Roger Kavanagh — 71 (unopposed)
- Moderator, 1-year term — Eugene Dellea — 81
- Board of Assessors, 3-year term — Susan Lupo — 72
- Planning Board, 5-year term — Sue Coxon — 72
- Finance Committee, 3-year term — Daniel Buehler — 71
- Cemetery Commission, 3-year term — Earl Moffatt — 67
- Finance Committee, 3-year term (previously appointed) — Frank Landsberger — 26
- Library Trustee, 1-year term — Gail Garrick — 75
- Board of Health, 1-year term — Earl Moffatt — 69
- Board of Health, 3-year term (vacant seat; no declared candidate) — Kathy Korte (write-in) — 55
