# Hubbardston, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Election, Tuesday, June 8, 2021

Source: the Town of Hubbardston's own results sheet, posted as an image to the
town's Facebook page,
https://www.facebook.com/photo?fbid=2930950747118731&set=a.224106483086521

The sheet is a single page on town letterhead, headed "COMMONWEALTH OF
MASSACHUSETTS / TOWN OF HUBBARDSTON / Annual Town Election, June 8, 2021 /
UNOFFICIAL Election Results", set in two columns. It carries the town seal.

The word UNOFFICIAL is the town's own, printed on the sheet, and is recorded here
rather than smoothed away. It is the ordinary election-night label: the counts are
the clerk's, taken from the tabulator, before the write-in acceptances below were
resolved.

## Turnout

Printed on the sheet, immediately under the heading:

"Total # Registered Voters = 3451    TOTAL VOTES CAST TODAY: 254 (7.36%)"

254 ballots cast of 3,451 registered voters. The printed percentage is correct:
254 / 3451 = 7.36 percent.

## Two contested races

Only two offices drew more candidates than seats:

  * Select Board, two seats, three candidates. Munroe 175 and Williams 161 were
    elected; Walker 74 was not.
  * Planning Board, one seat, three candidates. Kristofer Munroe 159 was elected
    over Jefts 62 and DeMalia 31.

Every other office was uncontested or drew no candidate at all.

## Two blanks that are blanks in the source

The Planning Board and the Trustees of the Public Library each print the label
"Write-Ins" with NO number beside it. That is how the sheet reads -- the value is
absent, not zero -- and it is left absent here rather than being inferred. The
Board of Assessors and Cemetery Commission lines, by contrast, do print an
explicit 0, so the distinction is the town's, not an artefact of reading the
image.

## Offices no one won on the ballot

Six of the twenty-one lines record no winner. Four Housing Authority seats, the
Board of Health's two seats, the Quabbin School Committee's two seats and the
Finance Committee seat drew only write-ins. The sheet marks these either "Failure
to Elect" (nobody reached the threshold) or "Pending Write-In Acceptance" (a
write-in led but had not yet accepted the office when the sheet was printed).
Whether those write-ins were later seated is not stated by this source and is not
asserted here.
