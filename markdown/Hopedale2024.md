ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION RESULTS
MAY 14, 2024
Hopedale, Massachusetts
In accordance with the posted warrant for the Annual Town Election, the inhabitants of the
Town of Hopedale qualified to vote in elections met in the Draper Gymnasium at 7:00 am with
Warden Ellen Murphy in charge of the election.
At 8:00 pm the polls were closed. The number of ballots agreed with the checklist, and it was
announced that the number of ballots cast was one thousand sixty- seven (1067) which was
26.4%. At the time of the election there were four thousand forty-two (4042) inhabitants
registered to vote.
The early voting ballots were processed during the polling hours. There were six hundred
(600) vote by mail applications and two hundred eighty seven (287) were returned. Seven
hundred eighty (780) residents voted at the polls.
