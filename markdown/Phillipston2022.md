# Phillipston, MA - Annual Town Election, May 16, 2022 - PARTIAL

Source: `May 16 2022 Election results.pdf`, attached to the **Town Clerk** meeting of
16 May 2022 on **mytowngovernment.org** (Phillipston's official minutes host, ZIP key
`/013310`). NOT published on phillipston-ma.gov - the town website's two election-results
DocumentCenter IDs (675, 811) are both dead.

The tally sheet is a **scan with no usable text layer**; recovered by 400 dpi OCR with
separate name-column and digit-only number-column passes joined by row position.

**Ballots cast: 113.** Not printed as a total - DERIVED, and independently confirmed by
six different offices each summing to exactly 113.
