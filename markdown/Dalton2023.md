ANNUAL TOWN ELECTION 
MAY 8, 2023
The Election was held from 11:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. at the Dalton Community House, pursuant to the Warrant on page 99. Two thousand Official Ballots were delivered to the Warden, and the Election Supervisor certified that the Ballot Boxes were empty and set at zero.  At the conclusion of the counting and tabulation, the Town Clerk announced that there had been a total of 122 Ballots cast as follows:
