# Leverett, Massachusetts -- Annual Town Meeting elections, April 29, 2023

Transcribed from the Town of Leverett's Annual Town Meeting minutes of 29 April
2023, https://leverett.ma.us/files/ATM_minutes_2023-4-29.docx

Leverett elects its officers on the floor of the Annual Town Meeting; no ballots
are cast and no vote counts exist. The meeting was called to order at the Leverett
Elementary School by Moderator Larry Farber at 9:11 am.

The results of the election held at that meeting are as follows. The minutes state:
"There were no contested elections. Newly elected officers:"

| Office | Term | Elected |
|---|---|---|
| Assessors | three years | Cat Ford |
| Board of Health | three years | Lizzie Alwan |
| Board of Health | three years | Cynthia Baldwin |
| Library Trustees | three years | Chris Condit |
| Library Trustees | three years | Joan Godsey |
| Constable | three years | John Swartz |
| Finance Committee | three years | Jed Proujanksy |
| Finance Committee | three years | Isaiah Robison |
| Planning Board | five years | Molly Daniel |
| School Committee | three years | Ann Elise Olson |
| Select Board | three years | Tom Hankinson |

The surname Proujanksy is spelled that way in the minutes and is transcribed as
printed.

No office was contested, and the minutes print no vote count for any office, so no
tally is recorded here.
