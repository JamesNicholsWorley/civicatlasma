The two eras are checked differently

The table above counts 2021–2026, where the source text was pooled line by line and every figure can be matched against the line it was printed on. The 2000–2020 records were read from page images and have no pooled lines, so those checks cannot run on them. What was checked there instead is the count of names and figures located on the page, which each record carries and the map reports for the town-year you are looking at.

A record only reaches the site at all after passing a publication gate. Nothing in json_pre2021/ is published unless that gate returned publish.

Why this page is not linked from the front

This is a working register for the people maintaining the corpus, not a disclaimer aimed at readers. Defects that touch a specific number are shown in the provenance drawer beside that number, which is where somebody reading a result will actually meet them. Putting the whole register on the front page tells a visitor that everything is suspect, which is both discouraging and untrue. Nothing here is hidden — it is filed where it is useful.
